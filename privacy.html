Privacy policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

clipfarm is a personal tool used only by its owner to upload videos to the owner's own YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API.

  • It requests only permission to upload videos to the owner's channel.
  • It does not collect, store, sell or share any data about other people.
  • The access token is stored only on the owner's own computer and is never shared.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

You can revoke access at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
